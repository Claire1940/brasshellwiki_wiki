<Section icon={Wrench} title="Why Upgrade Order Matters in BRASS HELL">

In BRASS HELL, every payout you earn from delivering crates is a choice: spend it on upgrades that compound, or hold it and fall behind. Because the game is an incremental factory simulator built around a repeatable loop — operate the press, craft rounds, fill crates, deliver — the upgrades that shorten that loop generate more earnings per session than upgrades that only add comfort. A wrong early purchase slows every cycle that follows; a correct one makes each later upgrade cheaper in relative terms.

The core principle is simple: **buy whatever removes your current bottleneck first**. If the press is idle while you gather materials, speed upgrades matter less than material handling. If crates fill faster than you can deliver them, throughput upgrades take priority.

| Bottleneck Symptom | What Is Slowing You Down | Upgrade Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Press sits idle between batches | Material collection and restocking | Handling / logistics upgrades |
| Crates fill faster than you deliver | Delivery and crate handling | Crate throughput upgrades |
| Long press cycles, fast shipping | Production speed | Press speed upgrades |
| Steady loop, low payout per crate | Order value | Shipment earnings upgrades |

<Callout type="tip" title="Editor's Rule of Thumb">
Watch one full production-to-delivery cycle before spending. Whatever step you spend the most real time waiting on is the upgrade category you should buy next.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step Upgrade Path">

Follow this sequence to take a fresh workshop from its first shipment to a self-sustaining factory. Each phase assumes the previous one is producing consistently.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish the Loop">
    Complete several production cycles without upgrading. Learn the press, crate area, and delivery route so you can identify your true bottleneck rather than guessing.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Buy Your First Speed Upgrade">
    Spend the first meaningful payout on production speed. Faster press cycles mean more completed crates per session, which accelerates every later purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Fix Crate and Delivery Throughput">
    Once the press outpaces your packing and delivery, invest in crate throughput. The goal is zero idle time between a finished batch and a dispatched shipment.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Scale Shipment Value">
    With a consistent loop, shift spending toward shipment earnings upgrades. Each delivery now pays more without requiring more of your time.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Unlock Advanced Ammunition">
    Use your improved income to progress toward ammunition unlocks. Higher-tier rounds raise the ceiling on both payout and long-term progression goals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Milestone Check">
If you can complete a full gather-press-pack-deliver cycle without waiting on any single step, your upgrade order is working — move to value scaling.
</Callout>
