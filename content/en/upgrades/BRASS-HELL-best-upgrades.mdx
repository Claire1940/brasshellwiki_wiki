<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Upgrade Path">

Follow this sequence when starting a fresh run. It balances safety with damage output so you never fall behind either curve.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Secure Defense First">
    Take the first health or damage reduction upgrade offered. This covers you against early burst damage and lets you play aggressively for resources.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Grab Economy Early">
    If a currency or reroll upgrade appears within the first few choices, take it. Extra economy compounds across an entire run.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Commit to a Damage Core">
    Pick one damage type and stack it. Splitting between physical and elemental scaling dilutes both and delays your power spike.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Add Attack Speed">
    Once base damage is solid, attack speed multiplies every hit effect you have stacked so far.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Fill With Synergy">
    Late choices should complete synergies: crit after damage stacks, lifesteal after Max HP, AoE after attack speed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Checkpoint Rule">
After every major upgrade, do one test fight. If your clear time dropped or you took unexpected damage, note which pick caused it and skip similar choices next run.
</Callout>

</Section>
