<Section icon={Package} title="Production and Shipment Efficiency">

Upgrades only matter when they improve the actions performed between one order and the next. The core BRASS HELL workflow includes gathering materials, operating the press, restocking inputs, checking the order, filling the crate, and dispatching the shipment.

Efficient players prepare the next action before the current task ends. While a crate is being finalized, check the next order. While materials are available, keep the press active. After dispatch, return directly to preparation instead of waiting in the delivery area.

| Factory step | What to do | Efficiency target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Materials | Review the active order and gather required inputs | Keep necessary materials near the production route |
| Press | Load inputs and complete the production interaction | Minimize unused press time |
| Restocking | Replace consumed materials before the next batch | Prevent avoidable interruptions |
| Crate preparation | Move finished ammunition into the correct container | Complete the full batch before traveling |
| Dispatch | Finalize and deliver the shipment | Start preparing the next order immediately |

### How to Identify Your Bottleneck

Track the portion of the cycle that causes the longest pause:

- If the press waits for materials, improve organization and restocking before changing ammunition goals.
- If production finishes before the crate is ready, prioritize handling or crate throughput.
- If the crate is completed quickly but payouts are modest, shipment earnings may provide better value.
- If the factory is efficient but progression feels slow, compare Rank Progress and Mastery XP activities separately.
- If multiplayer teammates divide tasks, assign one player to materials, one to production, and one to packing when practical.

### Free Progression Before Paid Support

The main factory loop remains the foundation of progression. Game passes may supplement a particular benefit, but they do not remove the need to operate equipment, manage materials, complete orders, and dispatch crates.

Before considering any paid option, establish these free habits:

- Keep the production area supplied.
- Complete full batches rather than making repeated partial trips.
- Compare the active order with the finished ammunition before packing.
- Reinvest shipment earnings into the upgrade that fixes your current delay.
- Check the current Roblox product description before making a purchase.

### Practical Upgrade Principles

The strongest general rule is to upgrade the slowest step, not necessarily the most impressive-looking option. A factory with excellent production speed can still underperform if the player spends too long gathering supplies or completing shipments.

Use these principles during regular play:

1. **Measure before changing direction.** Watch at least one complete order cycle.
2. **Prefer repeatable gains.** An upgrade that helps every shipment is usually easier to evaluate than a narrow convenience effect.
3. **Protect material flow.** Advanced ammunition is useful only when the factory can produce it consistently.
4. **Separate systems.** Mastery XP, Rank Progress, Bulk effects, and ordinary shipment income should not be treated as identical.
5. **Recheck after updates.** BRASS HELL is in Early Release during 2026, so workshop names, effects, and progression details may change.
