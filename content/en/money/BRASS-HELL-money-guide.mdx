<Section icon={Map} title="Advanced Wealth Strategies">

Once your basic loop is running, these strategies separate wealthy players from the rest:

- **Arbitrage awareness**: Monitor exchange or trading values if the mode is available. Price gaps between what players ask and pay can be exploited patiently.
- **Update timing**: Balance patches often shift which farm is best. Re-test your methods after every update before committing long sessions.
- **Opportunity cost thinking**: Before any purchase, ask what else that currency could buy. If the alternative raises income more, delay the purchase.
- **Community intel**: Official channels and community groups often announce bonus windows ahead of time. Staying informed is free money.

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Scams">
Never trade based on trust alone, and be skeptical of deals that look too good. If a trade requires you to send currency first with no guarantee, walk away.
</Callout>
