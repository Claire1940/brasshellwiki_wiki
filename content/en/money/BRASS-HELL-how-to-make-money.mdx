In BRASS HELL, your income comes almost entirely from manufacturing and selling ammunition. <Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step: Building a Profitable Ammo Line">

Follow this progression path to take your workshop from a manual bench into a fully automated money factory.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Master the Manual Loop">
    Load powder, seat bullets, and finish rounds by hand. Learn the rhythm of each cycle and sell your first boxes of ammo to build starting capital.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Reinvest in Machines">
    Spend your early earnings on loading machines. Even a hand loader speeds up cycles and lets you produce 50 rounds per run instead of one at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Unlock a New Caliber">
    Save up for a caliber unlock such as 7.62. Higher caliber rounds are worth significantly more per box, raising the ceiling of every sale.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Add Auto Press and Auto Packing">
    Purchase the auto press and auto packing machines. The powder pours and packs automatically, removing nearly all manual labor from the line.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Go Fully Automated and AFK Farm">
    With the line automated, production continues while you wait. Collect boxed ammo, sell in bulk, and reinvest in multipliers and upgrades.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch Your Supplies">
Automated lines consume supplies quickly. Running out of powder or components mid-cycle stalls the entire production line, so stock up on raw materials before going AFK for long sessions.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Common Money Mistakes to Avoid">

Even a good production line can leak profit if you fall into these traps.

- **Selling loose ammo** instead of waiting for full boxes slashes your income per run.
- **Ignoring caliber unlocks** keeps your per-cycle value low no matter how fast you produce.
- **Letting supplies run dry** on an automated line wastes AFK time with zero output.
- **Buying cosmetic upgrades first** delays the auto press and auto packing, which are the true income engines.
- **Skipping multipliers** after automation leaves easy money on the table.

<Callout type="warning" title="Repetitive Endgame Warning">
Once your line is fully automated, the loop becomes highly repetitive with little left to do besides collecting and selling. Set income goals before this point so you always have a next target to chase.
</Callout>
