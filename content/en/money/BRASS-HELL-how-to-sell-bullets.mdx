<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Selling Process">

Follow this sequence every time you plan a bullet sale. It maximizes the credits you receive while minimizing the risk of getting stranded without ammo.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Inventory Audit">
    Open your inventory and sort by ammunition. Check caliber, condition, and quantity. Set aside your reserve — the ammo you need for your current loadout — before anything else.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Sort and Stack">
    Group identical calibers and condition tiers into stacks. Vendors in BRASS HELL pay full listed price for clean, matched stacks and discount mixed, unsorted lots.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Check Vendor Prices">
    Visit at least two vendors before selling. Prices for the same caliber can vary noticeably between traders and shift with in-game supply conditions. Compare unit prices on identical stacks.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Sell to the Best Offer">
    Confirm the sale with the highest-paying vendor. Sell clean stacks first, then decide whether mixed leftovers are worth the discount or better saved for a later trip.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Reinvest Smartly">
    Convert a portion of your earnings into supplies, repairs, or better weapons before leaving the trading hub. Cash sitting idle earns nothing in BRASS HELL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Stack Discipline Pays">
Matched, sorted stacks consistently command better unit prices than loose mixed inventory. Spending two minutes sorting before a sale is one of the highest value-per-minute actions in the game's economy loop.
</Callout>

</Section>
