<Section icon={Swords} title="Step-by-Step: Building Your Money Factory">

Follow this progression path to go from your first box of rounds to a fully automated ammunition business.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Hand-Load Your First Rounds">
    Start by manually loading your starting caliber. Aim for perfect cycles — they give bonus output and train your rhythm. Sell every finished box immediately to build capital.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Buy the Bullet Multiplier">
    As soon as you can afford it, purchase the bullet multiplier. It raises the number of rounds produced per cycle and pays for itself quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Unlock a New Caliber">
    Expand to the next caliber (such as 7.62) with your earnings. New calibers sell in larger box quantities and raise your per-batch revenue significantly.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Order the Auto Press and Packing">
    Unlock Order Masters and order the auto press and auto packing. Once installed, powder pours, seats, and packs automatically — your line becomes fully hands-off.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Scale and AFK">
    Keep supplies stocked and let the automated line run. Sell completed batches — commonly in the tens of thousands, with mature lines exceeding **$100,000+ per batch** — and reinvest in further expansion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch Your Supplies">
Automated lines consume materials fast. If powder or components run dry, production halts and you lose AFK income. Check stock before stepping away.
</Callout>

</Section>
