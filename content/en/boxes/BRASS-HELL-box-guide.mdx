<Section icon={Map} title="Understanding the Box System in BRASS HELL">

Boxes are the primary loot distribution system in BRASS HELL. Each box contains a randomized pool of rewards — typically weapons, cosmetic gear, boosters, and rare variants of brass knuckles and other close-combat equipment. Because rewards are drawn from tiered pools, understanding which box to open at which stage of your progression is one of the most important efficiency decisions in the game.

The system works on a simple principle: higher-tier boxes cost more currency but pull from a reward pool with better floors. A low-tier box can still surprise you with a strong item, but over many openings, the expected value of premium boxes is significantly higher.

| Box Tier | Typical Cost | Reward Pool | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Box | Low | Common gear, small boosters | New players, early loadout |
| Standard Box | Medium | Uncommon gear, moderate boosters | Mid-game farming |
| Premium Box | High | Rare gear, cosmetic variants | Late-game optimization |
| Event Box | Event currency | Limited-time items | Collectors, meta shifts |

<Callout type="tip" title="Start Small">
New players should run several Basic Box openings first. Building a functional baseline loadout early beats chasing rare drops before you can actually use them.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Efficient Box Opening Routine">

Follow this routine to maximize the value of every opening session. The order matters — preparing before you spend prevents wasted currency on boxes you have outgrown.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Assess Your Loadout">
    Identify your weakest equipment slot. Open boxes that target that slot's reward pool rather than pulling randomly.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Set a Budget">
    Decide how much currency you will spend this session and stick to it. This prevents tilt-spending after unlucky pulls.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Open in Batches">
    Open boxes in small batches of 3 to 5. This gives you natural checkpoints to evaluate results and stop if you hit your target item.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Evaluate and Adjust">
    After each batch, check whether your weakest slot improved. If not, switch box types instead of doubling down.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Bank the Rest">
    Deposit leftover currency into your reserve for event boxes and future tier unlocks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Batch Discipline">
Players who open in controlled batches consistently report better long-term satisfaction than those who spend everything in one session — the stop condition protects your resources.
</Callout>
