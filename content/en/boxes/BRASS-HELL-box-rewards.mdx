<Section icon={Map} title="How the Box Reward System Works">

The puzzle box at the heart of the BRASS HELL mythos is not a single artifact but a **shape-shifting mechanism** with six distinct configurations. Each solved stage extends a blade that cuts the solver's hand, marking them for the Cenobites and claiming a sacrifice. Only a solver willing to sacrifice five humans reaches the final configuration and earns an audience with **Leviathan**, the god who rules the Labyrinth.

Once face to face with Leviathan, the solver may request one gift from six possible choices. The box's very structure telegraphs this: every physical form it takes represents one of the rewards on offer. Choose carefully, because the box does not offer refunds — only exchanges, and even those come with a brutal catch.

<Callout type="warning" title="The Marking Rule">
Every configuration you solve releases a blade that draws blood. That blood **marks you** as the Cenobites' target. The only escape is to have someone else take the cut — which is exactly how the box collects its victims.
</Callout>

A few patterns emerge from these cases:

- Gifts are granted **literally**, never mercifully — pain is technically a sensation.

- The only "win" recorded was refusing all supernatural rewards and choosing **Lament**.
