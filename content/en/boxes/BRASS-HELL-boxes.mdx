<Section icon={Package} title="What Are BRASS HELL Boxes?">

BRASS HELL boxes are the game's primary loot container system. They are awarded for completing encounters, clearing challenge tiers, finishing daily and weekly objectives, and purchasing them from in-game vendors with earned currency. Each box belongs to a tier, and that tier determines the quality range of the rewards inside.

Because progression in BRASS HELL leans heavily on gear and upgrade materials, understanding the box system is one of the fastest ways to accelerate a character's growth. New players often open every box immediately, but experienced players treat boxes as a resource to manage.

<Callout type="tip" title="Core Principle">
Boxes are not just rewards — they are a progression currency. Deciding **when** and **which** boxes to open is nearly as important as earning them in the first place.
</Callout>

The main characteristics of the box system:

- **Tier-based loot pools**: each tier has a fixed table of possible drops with weighted rarity.
- **Guaranteed minimums**: higher tiers include floor rewards so you never walk away empty-handed on key items.
- **Multiple sources**: encounters, objectives, vendors, and limited-time events all distribute boxes.
- **Inventory storage**: most boxes stack in inventory, letting you choose your opening timing.

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Farming and Opening Boxes">

Follow this workflow to turn box farming into a repeatable routine that supports steady character growth.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish Daily Encounters">
    Clear the repeatable encounters that reliably drop Standard boxes each day. Consistency here forms the baseline of your box income.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Stack Weekly Objectives">
    Work through weekly objectives that award Elite boxes. These are the highest-value routine sources and should be planned around your play schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Manage Your Stockpile">
    Hold higher-tier boxes in inventory while you level. Track your milestone levels so you know when a gear breakpoint is close.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Open at Milestones">
    When you hit a new progression tier, open your saved Elite and Reinforced boxes so the rewards roll against your improved gear curve.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Recycle and Reinvest">
    Dismantle duplicate or under-leveled gear from boxes into materials, then reinvest those materials into your best-equipped gear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Efficiency Loop">
The cycle of farm, stockpile, open at milestones, and dismantle duplicates creates a self-sustaining loop where every box contributes to progression — even the disappointing drops.
</Callout>
