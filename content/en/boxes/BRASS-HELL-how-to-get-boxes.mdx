<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Box Farming Route">

Follow this loop to maximize boxes per hour. It works from early game onward and scales as you upgrade.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish Your Grind Zone">
    Pick a wave area you can clear quickly without dying. Kill speed matters more than enemy level — a zone you clear twice as fast usually beats a harder zone.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Collect Every Box Immediately">
    Pick up boxes the moment they drop or use your in-game collection option. Boxes left on the ground after a wave reset can be lost.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Sell in Batches">
    Once your inventory fills, sell everything in one trip. Selling in batches keeps you in the farming zone longer per cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Reinvest Into Upgrades">
    Spend box income on pickup speed and storage first, then damage. Collection upgrades pay off on every future run.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Rotate Into Events">
    When an event phase opens, rotate there immediately, then return to your standard loop when it closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Loop Efficiency">
A tight sell-upgrade loop compounds: every upgrade shortens the next cycle, which means more boxes per hour without any extra effort.
</Callout>
