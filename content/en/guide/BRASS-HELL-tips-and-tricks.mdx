<Section icon={Map} title="Core BRASS HELL Tips for New Workers">

BRASS HELL drops you into a remote, snowbound ammunition factory where the entire income loop depends on how tightly you chain production steps together. The players who earn the most are not the ones who move fastest, but the ones who waste the least time between the press, the crate area, and the delivery point.

The factory supports up to **4 players per server**, so coordination matters as much as individual speed. Split roles between press operation, material hauling, and crate delivery when playing with a team.

| Tip | Why It Matters | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the press active | Idle machine time is lost income every cycle | Easy |
| Memorize material routes | Shorter walks mean more batches per shift | Easy |
| Batch before delivering | One trip per order cuts travel time sharply | Easy |
| Assign roles in co-op | Parallel work beats everyone doing everything | Medium |
| Reinvest every payout | Early upgrades compound across all later shipments | Easy |

<Callout type="tip" title="The Golden Rule">
Treat dispatch as the starting point of the next cycle, not the end of the shift. Immediately begin preparing materials for the next order the moment a crate leaves the floor.
</Callout>

Convenience-focused improvements such as movement and handling upgrades belong in the **B-tier**: useful, but only after the production loop is already efficient. Buying them early slows long-term income.

| Tier | Upgrade Type | Buy When | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | Production speed | Immediately | **More cycles per session** |
| S | Crate throughput | After first speed upgrade | Less downtime between batches |
| A | Shipment earnings | Mid-game, stable output | Higher payout per delivery |
| A | Ammunition unlocks | Late game planning | Access to advanced rounds |
| B | Convenience | Core loop covered | Smoother daily routine |

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid This Mistake">

<Section icon={Package} title="Efficient Production Cycle Walkthrough">

The core loop is simple to learn and hard to optimize. These BRASS HELL tricks turn a clumsy first shift into a repeatable, low-downtime routine.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Collect and Organize Materials">
    Review the active order requirements before touching the press. Move all required materials close to the production area so nothing interrupts the cycle halfway through.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Run the Press Continuously">
    Load materials, run the production interaction, and collect finished rounds. Return to the press immediately after collecting; an unused machine is wasted income.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Restock While Working">
    Replace consumed inputs as part of the loop, not as an emergency task. Use a fixed, repeatable route between the supply area and the press to cut walking distance.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Verify and Complete the Order">
    Compare finished products against the order before moving anything. Finish the full batch first so a single trip completes the entire delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Pack, Dispatch, and Restart">
    Load the crate, finalize the shipment, dispatch it, then instantly begin preparing the next order's materials. Dispatch is the beginning of the next cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Efficiency Benchmark">
If your press ever sits unused while you walk, restructure your route. The most efficient workers overlap material preparation with packing and delivery.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Rank, Mastery, and Ammo Progression Tricks">

BRASS HELL separates **Rank Progress** from **Mastery XP**, and the listed game passes (Mastery XP, Rank Progress, Bulk multipliers) each support a different system. Do not assume they stack into one combined multiplier unless an in-game description says so.

| System | How It Advances | Progression Trick |
|---|---|---|
| Mastery XP | Earned through continued factory activity | Keep completing production work consistently |
| Rank Progress | Earned through eligible production and shipments | Prioritize full, complete orders over partial runs |
| Bulk multipliers | Supports larger production output | Use bulk-oriented production when available |
| Factory work | Core loop: press, restock, pack, dispatch | Game passes supplement but never replace the loop |

For ammunition, the best caliber is generally the **highest unlocked round your factory can produce consistently** without slowing the full shipment cycle. Pushing into an advanced round before your press speed and material flow can support it often lowers total earnings.

| Stage | Round Type | Recommended Use |
|---|---|---|
| Starting production | Starter round | Learn the press, build first income loop |
| Early progression | Intermediate round | Adopt when workshop progression allows |
| Advanced progression | Advanced round | Primary option once speed and materials support it |

<Callout type="info" title="Pass Purchases">
Evaluate each progression pass on its own listed effect. Treat the in-game description as the authority rather than assuming multiplier stacking rules.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Document Hunting and Hidden Secrets">

The factory is more than a production floor. Scattered documents reward careful exploration and piece together the story behind the isolated facility. Search in pockets of downtime rather than interrupting active production cycles.

- **Production areas**: inspect corners, side tables, and shelves around the press, not just the main machine.
- **Offices and storage**: check desks, crates, and isolated rooms that normal shipment routes skip.
- **Hidden routes**: follow unusual openings that lead away from the standard loop.
- **Track your search**: use factory landmarks to mark which sections you have already cleared.

<Callout type="tip" title="Lore Tip">
Compare repeated names and locations across documents. Separate direct statements from environmental hints, and treat unresolved questions as open threads rather than confirmed lore.
</Callout>
