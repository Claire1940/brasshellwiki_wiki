<Section icon={Package} title="Beginner Walkthrough: Your First Production Cycle">

New workers should focus on completing one full cycle — press to payout — before experimenting with shortcuts. The loop below follows the route the factory is designed around.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Clock In at the Factory">
    Enter the facility and clock in to activate your work session. Walk the starting area to locate the press, crafting stations, crate zone, and delivery route before producing anything.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Operate the Press">
    Use the available press to begin manufacturing rounds. Follow the station prompts and repeat the production action until you have enough ammunition for a complete crate.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Craft Ammunition Rounds">
    Convert factory materials into finished rounds. Keep the production floor clear and continue crafting until the required crate quantity is met.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Fill the Shipment Crates">
    Carry finished rounds to the crate area and load them into the correct shipment containers. Double-check crate progress before leaving the floor.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Deliver the Shipment">
    Move the filled crate to the delivery point and complete the handoff. This first payout establishes the earnings loop that funds every future upgrade.
  </Step>
  <Step num="6" title="Reinvest in Upgrades">
    Spend early payouts on upgrades that shorten the production cycle or raise shipment value instead of banking cash. Reinvestment compounds across every future run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Efficiency Habit">
Prepare materials for the next order while the current shipment is being packed. The press should never sit idle between batches — treat dispatch as the start of the next cycle, not the end of the last one.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Ammo Progression: Choosing the Best Caliber">

Ammunition in BRASS HELL follows the workshop upgrade path. The best caliber is the highest round your factory can produce consistently without stalling the full shipment cycle.

| Unlock Stage | Caliber Tier | Production Value | Practical Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starting production | Starter round | Entry-level | Learning the press, first crates, building income |
| Early progression | Intermediate round | Improved | Raising shipment quality once basics are stable |
| Advanced progression | Advanced round | Highest tier | Maximizing returns in developed factories |

<Callout type="tip" title="Caliber Rule of Thumb">
A high-value round you cannot supply consistently earns less than a lower-tier round produced without interruptions. Match your caliber choice to your material flow, not just its listed value.
</Callout>
