<Section icon={Map} title="Core Gameplay Loop">

The gameplay revolves around a repeatable production cycle: clock in at the factory, operate the ammunition press, craft rounds, fill shipment crates, and deliver completed orders for payouts. Every delivery funds workshop upgrades that make the next cycle faster and more profitable.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Clock In at the Factory">
    Enter the remote factory and start your work session. Use the starting area to learn the layout: press, crafting stations, crate zone, and the shipment route.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Operate the Ammunition Press">
    Load materials into the press and repeat the production interaction until you have enough finished rounds for a full batch.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Craft Rounds">
    Convert factory materials into finished ammunition. Keep the production floor clear and continue until the crate requirement is met.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Fill Shipment Crates">
    Carry finished rounds to the crate area and pack them into the correct shipment containers. Check crate progress before leaving the floor.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Deliver the Shipment">
    Move the filled crate to the delivery point and complete the handoff to earn your payout.
  </Step>
  <Step num="6" title="Reinvest in Upgrades">
    Spend your first payouts on workshop improvements that shorten production cycles or raise shipment value rather than stopping after one delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Efficiency Tip">
Prepare materials for the next order while the current shipment is being packed. Treat dispatch as the end of one cycle and the starting point of the next — this keeps the factory from idling.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Ammunition and Workshop Upgrades">

Ammunition progression follows the factory upgrade path. Start with the round available at the beginning of your career, then advance toward better rounds as the workshop develops. The best caliber is generally the highest unlocked option your factory can produce **consistently** without slowing the full shipment cycle.

| Unlock Stage | Caliber Tier | Production Value | Best Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting production | Starter round | Entry-level | Learning the press, first crates, building income loop |
| Early progression | Intermediate round | Improved | Raising shipment quality once production is consistent |
| Advanced progression | Advanced round | Highest tier | Maximizing returns in developed factories |

For upgrades, priority ordering matters more than raw cost. Beginners should focus on improvements that shorten the production cycle, while advanced players balance throughput and shipment value.

| Tier | Upgrade Type | Benefit | Recommended For |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | Production Speed | More cycles per session | Beginners building income loop |
| S | Crate Throughput | Less downtime between batches | Players who produce faster than they ship |
| A | Shipment Earnings | Greater returns per delivery | Mid-game with stable supply |
| A | Ammunition Unlocks | Access to better rounds | Long-term progression planners |
| B | Convenience | Smoother daily routine | After core priorities are covered |

<Callout type="info" title="Upgrade Rule of Thumb">
Invest in upgrades that reduce the time from clock-in to dispatch before spending on convenience features. Faster loops compound across every future session.
</Callout>

| System | Purpose | How to Progress | Pass Note |
