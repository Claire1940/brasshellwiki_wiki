<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Progression Path">

Follow this sequence to avoid backtracking. Each step builds on the previous one, so resist the urge to skip ahead.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Complete the Tutorial and First Encounters">
    Finish every introductory encounter before exploring. These teach the combat rhythm and unlock the systems the rest of the game depends on.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Establish a Farming Routine">
    Identify one or two repeatable encounters that give consistent rewards. Run them daily to build a stable income of upgrade materials.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Commit to One Main Loadout">
    Spread too thin and every option stays weak. Concentrate resources on a single main build until it carries you into late content.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Push Advanced Areas">
    Once your main build holds up, unlock and clear the advanced zones. Expect a difficulty spike — adjust tactics rather than grinding endlessly.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Enter the Endgame Loop">
    Rotate between the hardest available encounters, refine your gear, and target the rare rewards that mark true completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Pacing Advice">
If an encounter takes more than a few attempts, step back and farm upgrades first. Brute-forcing hard content is almost always slower than strengthening your build.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Combat Fundamentals and Build Priorities">

Survival is the foundation of progression. A defensive-minded build clears content more consistently than a glass-cannon loadout, especially while you are still learning enemy patterns.

| Priority | What to Focus On | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Survivability** | Health, defense, dodge timing | You cannot win encounters you die in |
| **2. Consistent damage** | Reliable attacks, short cooldowns | Steady output beats burst you cannot land |
| **3. Utility** | Mobility, crowd control | Opens tactical options in tough fights |
| **4. Burst damage** | High-cost, high-reward options | Best saved for endgame optimization |

<Callout type="success" title="Rule of Thumb">
If you can clear an encounter without healing, your defense is sufficient — shift focus to damage. If you cannot, upgrade survivability first.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Resource Management and Daily Goals">

Resources are the real gatekeeper of progression. A disciplined daily routine compounds quickly, while scattered play leaves you perpetually short of upgrade materials.

| Resource Type | Best Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic materials** | Early and mid-game upgrades | Hoarding with no plan |
| **Premium currency** | Mid-to-late game unlocks | Tutorial-stage spending |
| **Rare drops** | Endgame gear and key upgrades | Low-impact sidegrades |
| **Time** | Repeatable high-value encounters | Replaying cleared easy content |

A practical daily loop looks like this:

| Daily Task | Approx. Time | Progress Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run core farming encounters | 15–20 min | ★★★★★ |
| Claim login / daily rewards | 2 min | ★★★★ |
| Attempt hardest clearable fight | 10 min | ★★★★ |
| Check official channels for updates | 3 min | ★★★ |
