<Section icon={Map} title="How to Play BRASS HELL: Core Gameplay Loop">

BRASS HELL is a bullet-manufacturing simulator built around one satisfying loop: gather components, load ammunition, sell finished rounds, and upgrade your operation until the entire factory runs itself. Every session starts with manual work at the loading bench and ends — if you progress far enough — with an automated assembly line producing boxes of ammo while you collect the profits.

The core resources you handle every cycle are straightforward, and understanding how they interact is the first step to playing efficiently.

| Resource | Role in Production | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Brass | Empty casing base for every round | Stock up in bulk, consumed fast |
| Black Seal | Functions like gunpowder | Core propellant ingredient |
| Bullets/Projectiles | Seated on top of each loaded round | Paired 1:1 with casings |
| Finished Rounds | Final sellable product | Boxed in batches of 50-60 |
| Cash | Earned from selling ammo boxes | Funds presses, automation, upgrades |

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: From First Round to Full Automation">

Follow this progression path to move from hand-loading newbie to automated factory owner as efficiently as possible.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Learn the Manual Loading Cycle">
    Place brass, pour powder (Black Seal), seat the bullet, and complete each round. Repeat until you can perform the cycle quickly and consistently. Early cash comes entirely from this manual work.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Sell in Bulk and Track Payouts">
    Box your finished rounds and sell them in batches. Watch your per-cycle earnings — bulk orders and multipliers can multiply a single sale's value dramatically.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Unlock Your Next Caliber">
    Save cash toward the next caliber unlock, such as 7.62. Higher calibers yield more rounds per cycle and significantly larger payouts per box.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Buy Presses and Loaders">
    Invest in a hand loader, then automated presses. Each machine removes a manual step from the cycle, letting you produce more rounds with less clicking.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Fully Automate and AFK">
    Purchase Order Masters, Auto Press, and auto-packing so powder pours, rounds assemble, and boxes pack without input. Clean up leftover manual machines and let the line run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch Your Supplies">
Automated lines drain supplies fast. A fully running factory can empty your material stock quickly — keep resource purchases ahead of production or the line stalls.
</Callout>

</Section>
