<Section icon={Map} title="Getting Started in BRASS HELL">

BRASS HELL is an incremental factory simulator on Roblox developed by earlymoto102. You work shifts in a remote, snowbound ammunition factory: operate the press, craft rounds, pack crates, and deliver orders while uncovering documents that reveal the site's story. The game entered early release in October 2026 and supports up to 4 players per server.

Before your first shift, understand the three pillars of progression:

| Pillar | What It Covers | Beginner Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Production | Operating the press, crafting rounds | Learn the press interaction and batch rhythm |
| Shipments | Crates, orders, delivery payouts | Complete full batches before delivery trips |
| Progression | Workshop upgrades, Mastery XP, Rank | Reinvest earnings into speed first |

<Callout type="tip" title="Start Smart">

<Section icon={Target} title="Your First Production Cycle: Step-by-Step">

The beginner route follows six steps. Repeat this cycle until the earnings fund your first workshop upgrade.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Clock In at the Factory">
    Enter the remote factory and clock in to activate your work session. Walk the starting area to locate the press, crafting stations, crate zone, and shipment route before producing anything.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Operate the Press">
    Use the available press and follow the station prompts. Repeat the production action until you have enough rounds for your first shipment requirement.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Craft Ammunition Rounds">
    Convert factory materials into finished rounds. Keep the production area clear and continue until the required quantity for a crate is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Fill the Shipment Crates">
    Carry finished rounds to the crate area and load the correct shipment containers. Verify crate progress before leaving the production floor.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Deliver the Shipment">
    Move the filled crate to the delivery point and complete the handoff. This first payout establishes the earnings loop that funds everything else.
  </Step>
  <Step num="6" title="Reinvest in Workshop Upgrades">
    Spend the payout on upgrades that make the cycle faster or increase shipment value rather than stopping after one delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid This Beginner Mistake">
Do not make a delivery trip with a partial batch. Finish the full order first so a single journey completes the shipment and saves travel time.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Materials, Restocking, and Efficiency">

Efficient players treat the factory as a continuous loop instead of a series of separate tasks. The biggest time sinks are walking and idle machines.

| Task | Common Waste | Efficient Habit |
|---|---|---|
| Material collection | Gathering mid-production | Prepare next order's materials during packing |
| Press operation | Machine sitting idle between batches | Return immediately after collecting output |
| Restocking | Waiting for shortages | Keep a small working supply near the press |
| Delivery | Multiple partial trips | Batch-complete orders, then deliver once |

<Callout type="success" title="Route Discipline">
Use a repeatable route between the supply area, press, and crate zone. Cutting walking time is one of the earliest free efficiency gains in the game.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Callout type="info" title="Ammunition Progression">
The best caliber is the highest one your factory can produce consistently without stalling the shipment cycle. Stick with the starter round while saving for upgrades, then adopt intermediate and advanced rounds as the workshop develops.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Co-op Play, Mastery, and Documents">

Servers support 4 players, so coordinate roles to multiply output.

| Role | Responsibility | Ideal Player |
|---|---|---|
| Press operator | Runs production continuously | Any consistent player |
| Material runner | Restocks inputs, preps next order | Fast, attentive teammate |
| Packer | Fills and finalizes crates | Detail-oriented player |
| Courier | Delivers shipments and returns | Player who knows the route |

Progression splits into **Mastery XP** and **Rank Progress**, advanced through continued factory work. Progression-related game passes exist for each system, but treat their effects separately unless in-game text explicitly combines them, and do not assume multiplier stacking rules.

Between cycles, explore. Documents are scattered through offices, storage rooms, and peripheral corridors away from the main loop. They piece together the factory's hidden story.

<Callout type="tip" title="Explore Between Orders">
Search corners, desks, shelves, and low-traffic routes after completing an order. Revisit areas you rushed past earlier once you know the layout better.
</Callout>

</Section>
