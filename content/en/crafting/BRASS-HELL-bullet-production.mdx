<Section icon={Factory} title="How BRASS HELL Bullet Production Works">

Bullet production is the core loop of BRASS HELL, a Roblox incremental factory simulator set in a remote, snowbound ammunition plant. You clock in, operate the ammunition press, craft rounds, fill crates, and dispatch shipments for payment. The faster and more consistently you repeat this cycle, the more you earn and the quicker you unlock workshop upgrades.

Every part of the loop feeds the next stage:

| Production Stage | Input | Output | Direct Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Material collection | Order requirements | Staged materials | Prevents press downtime |
| Press operation | Staged materials | Finished rounds | Core income source |
| Crate packing | Finished rounds | Filled crates | Required for delivery |
| Shipment dispatch | Filled crates | Payout | Funds upgrades |
| Restocking | Payout/stockroom | Next batch | Keeps the cycle moving |

<Callout type="tip" title="Editor's Note">
Treat dispatch as the end of one cycle and the starting point of the next — not a stopping point. Players who keep the loop moving continuously out-earn those who pause after each payout.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Map} title="Bullet Production Step-by-Step">

Follow this route for every production cycle. It works from your very first shift through late-game bulk runs.

| Step | Action | Efficiency Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Review the active order | Check quantities before pressing |
| 2 | Stage materials at the press | Missing parts interrupt the cycle |
| 3 | Run press batches | Return immediately after collecting output |
| 4 | Restock consumed inputs | Keep a small working supply nearby |
| 5 | Fill the shipment crate | Verify contents against the order |
| 6 | Dispatch and restart | Begin next-order prep instantly |

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Collect and Organize Materials">
    Gather everything the active order requires and keep it close to the press area. Identify anything that needs restocking before you start so shortages never interrupt production mid-batch.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Operate the Ammunition Press">
    Load materials, run the production interaction, and collect finished rounds. Keep the machine active whenever inputs are available — an idle press is the biggest efficiency loss in the factory.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Restock the Production Line">
    Replace consumed inputs after each batch. Use a repeatable walking route between the supply area and press to cut travel time across dozens of cycles.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Complete the Shipment Order">
    Match finished products against the order requirements and move them to the packing area. Missing or incorrect items block the entire loop from completing.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Pack and Dispatch the Crate">
    Load products, finalize the crate, dispatch the order, and immediately review the next order. Finish the full batch before making a delivery trip so one journey completes everything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid This Mistake">
Do not deliver partially completed batches. Multiple trips to the dispatch point for one order waste more time than any other common error in early production runs.
</Callout>

</Section>
