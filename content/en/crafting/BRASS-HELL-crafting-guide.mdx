<Section icon={Map} title="Crafting System Overview">

The crafting system in BRASS HELL is your primary path to endgame power. Enemies scale faster than world drops, so players who invest in workstations and material stockpiles consistently outperform those relying on random loot. Every crafted item can be further modified at upgraded benches, making crafting a two-stage process: forge the base item, then refine it.

**Core crafting pillars:**

- **Materials**: gathered from nodes, enemy drops, and salvage
- **Recipes**: unlocked through progression and exploration
- **Workstations**: benches that gate recipe tiers
- **Refinement**: post-craft upgrades that add modifiers

<Section icon={Target} title="Material Farming Priority">

Not all materials are equal. Your farming time should follow the rarity and consumption rate of each resource tier. The table below ranks the most valuable materials by effort-to-reward ratio.

| Material | Rarity | Primary Source | Used For | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brass Scraps | Common | Junk piles, basic enemies | Plating, low-tier weapons | ★★★ |
| Steel Chunks | Uncommon | Mine nodes, elite drops | Mid-tier weapons and armor | ★★★★ |
| Hellforged Alloy | Rare | Boss drops, deep-zone nodes | Endgame gear | ★★★★★ |
| Ember Dust | Uncommon | Salvaging enchanted items | Elemental modifiers | ★★★★ |
| Binding Resin | Uncommon | Plant nodes, traders | Attaching modifiers | ★★★ |

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Do not sell surplus Steel Chunks early in a run. Mid-tier recipes consume them in bulk, and buying them back from traders costs significantly more than the sale price.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Step-by-Step Crafting Progression">

Follow this sequence to build a crafting economy that carries you from the opening zones into the endgame without wasted resources.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish the Forge Bench">
    Complete the early workshop quest to unlock your first bench. Craft a basic brass weapon immediately so combat farming becomes faster.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Build the Salvage Station">
    Set up salvage before your inventory fills with unusable drops. Feed every spare item into it and stockpile Brass Scraps and Steel Chunks.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Craft Reinforced Brass Plating">
    This chest piece offers the best defense-per-material ratio in the early game and stays viable through the first two zones.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Upgrade to Tier 2 Recipes">
    Invest Steel Chunks into a mid-tier weapon. Damage scaling matters more than armor once enemy health pools grow.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Unlock the Alloy Furnace">
    Concentrate Hellforged Alloy runs in deep zones, then craft your first endgame weapon and add an elemental modifier with Ember Dust.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Checkpoint">
By the end of Step 5 you should have a Tier 3 weapon, a full mid-tier armor set, and a growing modifier stockpile — the standard benchmark for endgame readiness.
</Callout>

</Section>
