<Callout type="warning" title="Gauge Numbers Run Backwards">
Shotgun gauges get **bigger as the number gets smaller** — 10 gauge is larger than 12 gauge. Don't overspend expecting the higher number to be the upgrade.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Material Consumption">
Upgrading rounds per cycle does **not** create rounds from nothing. Five rounds per cycle consumes up to five brass or copper per cycle, so keep material storage topped up.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step Production Workflow">

Follow this loop every session to keep the factory running at peak efficiency:

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Stock Materials">
  Buy brass, copper, and powder from the stores. Speak to Ilya at the powder stores when restocking. Bigger material capacity (up to 1,500) means fewer trips.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Set Your Batch">
  Load the tray with brass shells. Choose your highest-value unlocked caliber for maximum income per cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Add Powder Precisely">
  Drag powder to match the meter exactly — sloppy pours waste material. Once you buy auto powder, this step disappears.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Seat and Press">
  Drop the projectile tip into the tray, then work the press lever. With auto press installed, the lever finishes rounds for you.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Seal and Deliver">
  Finished rounds load into cases automatically with auto packing. Seal full boxes, deliver to the buyer, and reinvest the payout into your next upgrade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Sell in Bulk">
Wait for **large sealed batches** before delivering. A single 1,600-round sealed delivery can be worth around $100,000 — far more efficient than constant small trips.
</Callout>
