<Section icon={Map} title="Ammo Maker Basics: How the Factory Works">

In BRASS HELL, you play as a factory worker on the ammunition production line. The core loop is simple but demands precision: take brass, press the casing, drag the powder, seat the projectile, and seal the finished round. Each completed batch goes into an output box, and delivering boxes to the counter converts your labor into cash.

**Video Highlights:**
- Manual production loop shown from the very first .22 round
- Powder and press upgrades demonstrated step by step
- Caliber unlocks from .22 all the way to 20 mm and 30 mm
- The factory's mysterious 4:17 a.m. time-loop story beats

Your early income is tiny — single rounds sell for cents. The whole progression arc of the game is about reinvesting those small profits into machines and powder so that one press cycle eventually spits out an entire box of rifle ammunition.

<Callout type="tip" title="Precision Matters">
The powder step cannot be rushed. You need to measure the correct amount into each casing — a sloppy scoop will not register as a completed round. Slow down on powder, speed up everywhere else.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="Best Upgrade Order: Step-by-Step">

Not all upgrades are equal. Prioritizing powder multipliers and batch capacity over cosmetic buys keeps your income curve steep.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Early Game: Double Output">
    Buy the two-rounds-per-cycle upgrade immediately after your first .22 sales. Doubling manual output halves your time per box more than anything else at this price range.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Powder Multipliers First">
    Upgrade powder at every opportunity. Early multipliers scale round value by 1.5x, then 2.5x, then 4x, and eventually 7x — this compounds with every caliber you unlock.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Bulk Storage and Batches">
    Invest in bigger shell storage, a larger output box, and brass dispensers. Bigger batches mean fewer trips to the delivery counter per dollar earned.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Hire and Automate">
    Unlock the helper, auto powder, auto press, and auto packing. Each automation removes one manual step until you are only pressing the lever.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Endgame: Auto Bench">
    The lever bench automatically finishes rounds — at this point production runs itself between deliveries, and sealed inventories reach 12,000+ rounds worth millions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
