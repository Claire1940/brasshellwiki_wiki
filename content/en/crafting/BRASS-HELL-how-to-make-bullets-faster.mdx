Every new BRASS HELL player hits the same wall: hand-loading rounds one inspection at a time. Early on, you check bullets in small batches, often around **10 out of 60 rounds** per inspection cycle, and the pace feels glacial. Once you understand that the game rewards reinvestment, the solution becomes obvious—your money should go straight into machines that replace you.

One frequently reported symptom is a line that was fast, then suddenly crawls. In most cases this is simply an empty supply hopper—your machines are fine, they just have nothing to work with. The second most common cause is forgetting that a new caliber changes your production requirements, which can temporarily slow things down until you restock matching components.
